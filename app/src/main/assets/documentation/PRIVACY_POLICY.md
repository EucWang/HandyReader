# Privacy Policy

*Effective Date: 22-04-2026*

Welcome to Handy Reader. This Privacy Policy is designed to help you understand how we handle policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information if anyone decided to use our App.

## Information We Collect and use

Handy Reader is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not directly collect or store any personal information from our users for app functionality. However:

- The app requires access to your device's storage to retrieve locally stored book files.
- We store user preferences locally on your device using data stores.
- When you submit feedback through our web form, we collect your email address, name (optional), feedback type, content, app version (optional), and source platform to process and respond to your feedback.
- The app provides Text-to-Speech (TTS) functionality. All text-to-speech processing is performed entirely on your device — no text content is sent to any server.

## Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party advertising services or analytics that collect user data. The TTS functionality uses sherpa-onnx, an open-source on-device speech engine. No user data is sent to third parties through this component.

## Use of Information

We do not collect or use any personal information directly for app functionality. The app accesses your device storage solely to provide the core functionality of reading ebooks stored on your device. Feedback information is used only to process and respond to user inquiries and improve our services.

## On-Device Text-to-Speech

Handy Reader includes a built-in Text-to-Speech feature powered by **sherpa-onnx**, an open-source speech processing framework (licensed under Apache License 2.0). This feature uses locally stored neural network models to convert text from your books into spoken audio.

- **All processing happens on your device.** No text content or audio data is transmitted to any external server.
- **Model downloads:** TTS voice models are downloaded from our server to your device for local use. Once downloaded, models operate entirely offline on your device.
- **No data collection:** We do not collect, transmit, or store any text you convert to speech, nor the generated audio.

## Data Sharing

We do not share any user data with third parties. Feedback information is stored securely in our database and is not shared with external parties.

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect the information that is stored locally on your device, such as your reading preferences.

## Children's Privacy

Handy Reader is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Effective Date" at the top.
